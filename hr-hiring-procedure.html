Hiring Procedure
Step-by-step guide for managers
1
Application Form
›

Every candidate must fill out the application form completely, including Employment History with at least 1 reference.

⚠ No Social Security number should be written anywhere on the application form.
2
Identity & Work Authorization Check
›

Verify that the Social Security number, legal name, and work authorization all match the applicant's IDs.

If SS card is not available, write a signed statement confirming the information is accurate and have them sign next to it.
3
Interview (Management)
›

Conduct the interview. Explain the tips policy clearly during this conversation.

4
Complete Forms & Copy ID
›

Have the new hire complete the following and provide a copy of their ID:

  • W-4 — Federal Tax Withholding. Check: readable · start date top-right corner · SSN format xxx-xx-xxxx (NOT ITIN starting 9xx/8xx/7xx) · DOB · address/phone/email · marital status · claim # · signature & date
  • I-9 — Employment Eligibility Verification
5
Handbook Training (2 Hrs Paid)
›

2 paid hours — conducted by management or self-directed. Added to next paycheck. Cover all of:

  • Food safety
  • Safety training
  • Harassment training
  • Tips policy
  • Benefit packages
  • Q&A form — employee's own handwriting, notes on back
  • Sign the handbook + acknowledgement form
6
Food Handler Card / ServSafe
›

Employee must provide a valid Food Handler Card or ServSafe certification within 30 days of their start date.

7
RBS / ABC Certificate
›

All FOH employees must have a valid RBS/ABC certificate before their first day.

Mandatory for anyone hired after September 2022.
8
Vacation & Availability Check
›

Ask the new hire if they have any planned vacations or conflicts and note them.

9
Sick Pay Policy
›

Sick pay accrues from day one (1 hr per 30 hrs worked). Can be used after 90-day probation.

No cash-out policy after termination. Requires doctor's note. Cannot be paid in credits or cash.
10
Discipline & Incident Reports
›

No verbal warnings. All discipline is documented via incident reports for the record only. Do not acknowledge the report to the employee.

11
Resignation & Termination
›

Always request a resignation letter when an employee voluntarily quits. Get it in writing.

12
Employment Verification
›

For loan, housing or other verification requests:

  • All employees are AT-WILL — no permanent position
  • Do NOT disclose part-time or full-time status
  • You may share average hours worked per week from records
