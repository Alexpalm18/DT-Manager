New Employee Q & A
Have them answer in their own handwriting
📝 Use this as a reference when reviewing answers.
Employee completes the physical form — not this screen.
1. What is your employment status at Daughter Thai Kitchen?
A: "At-will" — both you and Daughter Thai Kitchen have the same right to terminate employment at any time with or without cause or notice.
2. How often will you receive a paycheck?
A: Every other week.
3. For an unscheduled absence, how many hours before your shift must you notify the Manager on Duty?
A: 2 hours.
4. Employee Leave Requests should be submitted at least how many weeks in advance?
A: 2 weeks.
5. What are the holidays for Daughter Thai Kitchen?
A: We do not have regular scheduled holidays. It is management's direction.
6. How many hours worked earns you 1 hour of paid sick leave?
A: 30 hours.
7. Sick leave can be capped at up to how many hours?
A: 80 hours.
8. Are you allowed to clock in or out for another employee?
A: No.
9. Are you allowed to drink alcohol, gamble, or use drugs inside the restaurant or before, during, or after your shift?
A: No.
10. Are you allowed to use your cell phone during work?
A: No — only for emergencies and only with manager authorization.
11. What would you do if you were involved in a robbery?
A: Do not resist. Your safety first.
12. How many seconds is the hand-washing process?
A: 20 seconds.
13. What would you do if you saw water on the floor?
A: Wipe up spills immediately.
14. What kind of shoes must you wear when working?
A: Non-slip shoes.
15. When faced with a customer complaint, what would you do?
A: Don't get defensive. Apologize for the problem and tell the customer you will take care of it. Notify the Manager on Duty. Never hesitate to ask management when a problem arises.
